## Composition and exports

Retain actual returned raster originals and revision history. PNG/WebP exports replace former native-SVG requirements. Export dimensions in the registry are final design targets; verify supported request dimensions and then apply only safe mechanical crops or resizes. Use central safe zones until actual platform-crop tests establish the placement. Light/dark artwork is generated or edited through gpt-image-2, not programmatically recolored.

Icon sheets may be sliced only along reviewed boundaries into unchanged generated symbols. Fix a bad symbol through gpt-image-2 before slicing. Avatar/favicons must be checked at 16–48 px, thumbnails at 320 px, mobile artwork in a narrow viewport, and transparent overlays on contrasting backgrounds. Motion consists of generated stills/layers/frames mechanically assembled; every visible art element carries generation lineage. gpt-image-2 is not assumed to produce video/audio. Keep captions, readable factual UI, and a reduced-motion still as separate accessible content. Raw gameplay footage remains authentic source media, not generative video.
