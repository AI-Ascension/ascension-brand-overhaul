# Bounded external name collision review

Reviewed: 2026-09-07 02:02 EDT (2026-09-07 06:02 UTC)
Owner: W01-L
Purpose: ordinary obvious-name preflight for the proposed **AI Ascension**, **Ascension**, and **The Climb — by AI Ascension** names.

This is a bounded search for obvious external confusion signals. It is not trademark, domain, corporate-name, or legal clearance. The selected symbol was not reviewed because the generated identity artwork is not available; symbol review remains blocked until the W02 Astra and `gpt-image-2` asset has provenance and can be inspected.

## Method

The web search interface was used with these exact queries on the review date:

1. `"AI Ascension" organization project`
2. `"Ascension" AI game tooling`
3. `"The Climb" AI series`
4. `site:github.com "AI Ascension"`

First-party or primary pages were opened where available. The search was limited to obvious textual and category overlap; it did not attempt a global index, registration search, or jurisdiction-specific analysis.

The results did surface clear collisions for the shorter names and adjacent AI/game category, so the copy should keep the organization qualifier visible.

## Copy decision

- Keep **AI Ascension** as the organization identity, the `AI-Ascension` GitHub handle, and the `aiascension.tech` domain.
- Pair **Ascension** with “by AI Ascension” or “Slay the Spire 2” in page titles, repository descriptions, and external metadata unless the surrounding context already names AI Ascension.
- Use **The Climb — by AI Ascension** as the series name. Avoid presenting “The Climb” alone as the project’s show or channel name.
- Keep the independent-project and game-specific disclosures. Do not imply affiliation with any similarly named project, show, game, or company.
- Re-run the textual review after any name, handle, domain, or product-scope change and perform the symbol review after the generated mark is available.

## Limits

The review did not query trademark offices, company registries, domain registries, app stores, social platforms, paid search databases, or every jurisdiction. It did not assess likelihood of confusion, ownership, rights, or registration eligibility. No legal clearance is claimed, no domain or registration was purchased, and no external settings were changed.
