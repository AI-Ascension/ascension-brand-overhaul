## Pinned community introduction

Welcome to AI Ascension and The Climb.

Start with one source-linked run report. Ask what the agent observed, which action settled, what the game reported, which interventions occurred, and whether the public artifact is a report, video, or replay package.

Please do not post credentials, private saves, raw trajectories, hidden prompts, or personal contact details. A local guess-and-reveal is not a community vote. A source or test result is not automatically host, gameplay, deployment, or release proof.

Useful participation:

- propose one bounded, reproducible question;
- annotate a reviewed public decision with consent;
- improve a contributor task or documentation path;
- report a correction with the exact source revision.

Credit contributors accurately. Keep model/provider disagreement tied to evidence and moderate personal attacks, fabricated results, referral spam, and engagement manipulation.

## Press boilerplate

AI Ascension is an independent project building open tooling for game-playing-agent experiments, starting with Slay the Spire 2. Its flagship toolkit, Ascension, separates game-process boundaries, control routing, agent coordination, recorded actions, and replay or publication evidence. The Climb is a recurring series that turns each approved attempt into an inspectable report or, when rights and artifacts allow, a video or replay package.

As of the 2026-09-07 source refresh, public source reports describe a Windows STS2 v0.107.1 practice campaign reaching Defeat on floor 17 after 333 settled operations and a Linux practice campaign reaching Defeat on floor 24 after one controller restart and 431 settled operations. Both are maintainer-reported report-only artifacts; they are not a verified win, a matched benchmark, or proof of broad compatibility. AI Ascension does not claim endorsement by Mega Crit or Valve.

## Concise pitch

AI Ascension makes agent gameplay inspectable: the audience can watch a bounded attempt, follow the recorded decision, and read the source and limits behind it. The first season is still working toward a verified win, so the story is the evidence and the next question, not a promised result.
