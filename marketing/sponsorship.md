# Sponsorship and support standard

Status: policy draft. No sponsor, paid asset, gift, creator compensation, advertisement, or sponsorship arrangement is active. This package requests no budget and performs no purchase.

## Principles

- Support cannot purchase a benchmark outcome, favorable review, model ranking, placement, moderation decision, or publication timing.
- Every material support relationship is disclosed beside affected content and in the source index.
- A sponsor does not receive private credentials, hidden reasoning, raw trajectories, saves, or game-control access through the marketing program.
- Editorial ownership, source attribution, correction handling, and withdrawal paths remain with AI Ascension and the named source/creator owners.
- A sponsorship claim is not a gameplay, deployment, popularity, or legal-clearance claim.

## Allowed support, only after explicit approval

A future operator may consider a written, budget-approved arrangement for a clearly scoped service or resource, such as:

- time-boxed creator annotation;
- hosted test infrastructure with documented access and retention;
- accessibility or transcription review;
- a disclosed production service.

The agreement must state amount or in-kind value, dates, deliverable, access boundary, data retention, attribution, disclosure placement, cancellation, and correction/withdrawal terms. It must not require a favorable result, positive statement, star/follow/share, private data handoff, or exclusivity that obscures conflict.

No ads, domain purchases, paid assets, gifts, or creator compensation are authorized by this kit.

## Mandatory disclosure

Place this beside the content and in the campaign record:

> Sponsored support: [supporter] provided [specific support] during [window]. It did not purchase placement, determine the result, or condition publication on a favorable outcome. The affected content is [content ID], and its evidence state is [report_only/video_available/replay_package_available]. Source and measurement limits are listed here.

If the support affects a creator or reviewer, add:

> [Creator/reviewer] received [specific support]. Their commentary is their own and is separated from the recorded source facts. Quotation and attribution were approved for [scope]; corrections and withdrawal requests go to [public route].

Do not say “partner,” “sponsor,” “endorsed,” or “independent” unless the agreement and review support that exact word.

## Disclosure review

Before any commitment or publication:

- [ ] operator authority and budget are recorded;
- [ ] the recipient is identified through a public, consented route;
- [ ] scope and value are documented;
- [ ] support cannot change a result, denominator, exclusion, or correction;
- [ ] source owner, creator, and sponsor roles are distinct;
- [ ] private data and credentials are excluded;
- [ ] disclosure appears beside the affected item;
- [ ] attribution and correction/withdrawal rights are accepted;
- [ ] independent reviewer signs the exact disclosure;
- [ ] result and audience metrics remain unobserved until collected.

## Measurement

Mark sponsorship as a campaign configuration field and record the affected content IDs. Compare supported and unsupported content only under a reviewed protocol; do not infer lift from a single post. Use aggregate events and declared denominator, window, bot/test filter, and privacy retention. If the support changes audience, placement, or asset availability, record it as a confounder.

## Prohibited arrangements

Reject or hold any arrangement that:

- asks AI Ascension to hide or alter a defeat, restart, intervention, failure, or correction;
- promises a verified win, benchmark superlative, 24/7 autonomy, universal compatibility, or broad support;
- trades access to artifacts for engagement;
- requests raw private runtime artifacts or secret credentials;
- targets people with bulk unsolicited outreach;
- presents a generated poster as gameplay evidence;
- claims Mega Crit, Valve, a model provider, or a creator endorses the project without explicit evidence.

## Record

A future support record belongs in the private approvals system, not this public marketing kit. This repository stores only the approved disclosure and sanitized content link. Until an operator records authority, all sponsorship state is unobserved and all drafts remain unsent.
