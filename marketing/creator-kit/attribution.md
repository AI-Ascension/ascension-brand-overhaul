# Attribution and disclosure standard

## Source facts

Use the source repository, exact revision, evidence kind, and observed date. Preserve the original source owner. A maintainer-reported run is labeled as such; a public replay is labeled as a replay; an independently reproduced result requires its own evidence.

## Commentary

Label creator commentary as commentary. Do not turn an interpretation into a recorded action or outcome. Let the creator approve exact quotations, name/handle, link, and context. Preserve corrections and withdrawal requests.

## Contributions

Credit the contributor, reviewed diff, source revision, command, and code-owner review. A passing static check remains source/test evidence; it is not host or gameplay proof.

## Material support

Place the sponsorship disclosure beside affected content and in the source index. State what support was provided and that it did not purchase placement or condition publication on a favorable result. See sponsorship.md.

## Engagement

Never require stars, follows, shares, referrals, votes, or public praise for access to artifacts, review, or contributor recognition.
