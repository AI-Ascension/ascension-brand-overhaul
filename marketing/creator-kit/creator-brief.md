# Creator annotation brief

Status: draft commissioning brief; not an agreement.

## Purpose

Invite a creator or reviewer to inspect one source-linked decision, protocol, or contributor task. The external voice must stay visibly separate from recorded facts. A creator may disagree, identify missing information, or decline publication.

## Access boundary

Provide only approved public reports, source revisions, sanitized manifests, and the exact question. Do not provide private trajectories, raw captures, saves, credentials, account tokens, hidden reasoning, private prompts, or game-control endpoints. If a source artifact is not public and rights-reviewed, the request pauses.

## Requested deliverable

Choose one:

- one 20–30 minute recorded or written annotation of a reviewed public decision;
- up to three corrections to a replay/evidence explanation;
- a written review of the comparison protocol's denominator and exclusions;
- one review of a bounded good-first contribution task.

No deliverable includes a promise of a win, ranking, reach, or favorable coverage.

## Recording and publication

The default is no recording and no publication until written consent. If a recording is approved, the evidence owner records the file's rights, scope, retention, and public artifact state. The final content includes:

- the exact source URL and revision;
- the commentator's public name/handle as approved;
- the fact layer, commentary layer, and uncertainty layer;
- any intervention or missing context;
- a correction and withdrawal path;
- disclosure of any material support.

## Attribution

Attribute the creator's words to the approved name and link their public channel when requested. Do not imply employment, partnership, endorsement, sponsorship, or review of the entire product. Attribute source facts to the source owner and revisions. Attribute an implementation change to its contributor and code-owner review.

## Measurement and privacy

Track only consented aggregate events such as meaningful_watch_or_read, evidence_open, and build_start. Never store creator email addresses in marketing events. Retain the minimum aggregate window needed for the stated experiment and delete raw contact notes from local working storage according to the approved operator process.

## Acceptance

The creator artifact is ready only after consent, source/rights review, correction path, and independent editorial review. Until then, label the slot and result unobserved.
