# W06 launch and 90-day operating kit

This directory is a complete authored planning kit for The Climb. It contains twelve relative weeks, twenty-four distinct short clips, six technical article outlines, launch copy, a report-only path, creator drafts, community journeys, sponsorship standards, and bounded experiments. It does not claim campaign operation, audience results, creator responses, public video, replay package, or delivered artwork.

- [Machine-readable calendar](calendar.json) is the source of truth for item fields and counts.
- [Launch package](launch/) includes actual-capture-ready and report-only first-episode scripts, qualified copy variants, digest, posts, bios, press language, and corrections.
- [Creator kit](creator-kit/) contains personalized draft outreach, a time-boxed request, attribution, consent, and withdrawal boundaries.
- [Community playbook](community-playbook.md) defines spectator, contributor, annotator, challenge, correction, and moderation journeys.
- [Experiments](experiments.json) define seven tests with numerator, denominator, window, stopping, sample/qualitative protocol, confounders, privacy, and unobserved result fields.
- [Sponsorship standard](sponsorship.md) prohibits undisclosed influence and unapproved spend.
- [Source map](source-map.json) maps public facts to exact revisions and evidence classes.
- [Asset dependency record](asset-dependency.json) resolves all 72 registry IDs and records their blocked status.

Relative weeks remain unoperated until an approved launch date, source refresh, rights review, destination check, and independent content review exist.
